# Dashboard (staff + admin) — surface brief

Scope: whole-app visual refresh (identity redesign + every surface), landing first on the
staff and admin dashboards. Mode: **Operate**.

Audience/job: staff glance at their store's day on a phone between customers — sales,
credit owed, items in stock. Admin oversees both stores at once — daily sales per store,
overall sales, credit, payments, stock, received stock.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The dashboards refuse the category default of a neutral card grid. They live in
the painted-delivery-vehicle world — enamel paint, route-board headers, stencil plates —
so a store's day reads the way a route board does: one big figure, two store columns, a
persistent now.

OWN-WORLD: Saturated factory-paint panels with hard edges, no gradients, on a warm
workshop-wall ground. Bold painted route-board capitals for headers and plates; clean
grotesque for body; tabular numerals for every figure. A persistent now-mark on today's
date. Every zone labeled by its literal name. Named balance states (healthy / attention /
critical) for credit and stock.

STORY: Staff see their store's day as a painted plate readable across the shop — today's
sales as one enamel number, credit and stock as named plates. Admin sees Amara and Zeann
as two painted columns of the same depot board, shared credit as a hanging tag. Store
context never leaves the frame.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Staff — a route-board header naming the store; today's sales as the one
dominant enamel panel; below, two named stencil plates for outstanding credit and items in
stock; now-mark on the date strip; 44px touch targets; mobile-first. Admin — overall daily
sales as the dominant panel; Amara and Zeann as two painted columns with their day
figures; shared credit as a hanging tag with a named balance state.

FORM: painted vehicle signage (grounded candidate 5 of 7). Seed key: f161c156.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review,
the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
